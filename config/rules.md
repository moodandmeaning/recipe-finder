# Recipe rules

A recipe goes into the app only if it passes **every** rule below. When in doubt, reject it.

## The goal
Very healthy, whole-food cooking. Kosher. No dairy. No grains.

## Allowed ingredients
- Vegetables and fruits (fresh, frozen, or dried without added sugar)
- Legumes: lentils, chickpeas, beans, peas, edamame. Tofu and tempeh are fine.
- Kosher meat (beef, lamb, veal, turkey) and chicken
- Kosher fish: fish with fins and scales (salmon, tuna, cod, sea bass, trout, sardines, etc.)
- Eggs
- Nuts and seeds, nut butters without added sugar, tahini
- Herbs, spices, garlic, ginger, lemon, vinegar, mustard
- Healthy fats in moderate amounts: olive oil, avocado oil, avocado
- Natural sweetness from fruit, dates or date syrup (silan), in small amounts
- Plant milks (unsweetened): coconut milk, almond milk
- Potatoes and sweet potatoes, baked/roasted/boiled (not fried)

## Reject the recipe if it contains
1. **Dairy**: milk, cheese of any kind, butter, ghee, cream, sour cream, yogurt, labneh, kefir, whey, casein.
2. **Anything not kosher**: pork, bacon, ham, shellfish (shrimp, crab, lobster, mussels, clams, squid), fish without fins and scales (catfish, eel, shark), rabbit. Do not combine meat or chicken with fish in the same dish.
3. **Grains of any kind**: wheat, white or whole-wheat flour, bread, breadcrumbs, pasta, couscous, bulgur, rice (white or brown), oats, quinoa, barley, corn/cornmeal/polenta, buckwheat, millet, freekeh, spelt. Recipes that list grains as an optional side (e.g. "serve with rice") are OK only if the dish itself is complete without them. In that case drop the grain from the ingredients and steps and mention it in `whyFits`.
4. **Added refined sugar**: white or brown sugar, powdered sugar, corn syrup, sweetened condensed milk. Large amounts of honey or maple syrup (more than 2 tablespoons for the whole recipe) also count.
5. **Deep frying.** Pan-searing or light sautéing in a little oil is fine.
6. **Processed foods**: ready-made sauces (ketchup, BBQ sauce, store-bought dressings), bouillon/soup powder (אבקת מרק), margarine, sausages, hot dogs, deli or cured meats (pastrami, salami), schnitzel coating, canned fruit in syrup, artificial sweeteners. Plain canned beans, canned tomatoes and canned tuna are fine. **Exception (the user's rule):** mayonnaise is allowed in salad dressings (homemade or avocado-oil mayo preferred). Mayo is still not used outside salads.
7. **Unhealthy amounts of oil or salt**: more than about 1 tablespoon of oil per serving.

## Desserts: extra strict (the user's rule)
Only truly healthy sweets are allowed: **smoothies and shakes, fruit platters and fruit salads, date/nut balls and stuffed dates**, and similar no-bake treats made from whole fruit, nuts and seeds.
- **No added sweetener at all**, so no sugar, honey, maple, date syrup, agave or chocolate chips, not even "optional". Sweetness may only come from whole fruit and whole dates.
- **No flour of any kind**, including almond, coconut, tapioca and chickpea flour.
- **No baked goods**: no cakes, brownies, muffins, cookies, breads, pancakes, pies or bars that need baking.

## Kinds of dishes we want (keep each day varied)
`salad`, `soup`, `chicken`, `meat`, `fish`, `vegetables` (vegetable mains), `legumes`, `eggs`, `pashtida` (kugels and crustless egg-and-vegetable bakes: no flour, matzah meal, potato starch, cheese, soup powder or mayo), `dessert` (healthy, no refined sugar).

## Other preferences
- No time limit. Always record the times when the source gives them.
- Mark `shabbat: true` when the dish keeps well and can be made a day ahead or kept warm (stews, roasted chicken, many salads, soups).
